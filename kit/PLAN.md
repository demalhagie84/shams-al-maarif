2. Real recitation only, from openly licensed sources (EveryAyah, cdn.islamic.network), uploaded to this artifact's asset store and logged in kit/audio.json. Names play from a recited āya that contains them; non-Qurʾānic duʿāʾs show vowelled Arabic + transliteration until Al records them.

4. Beautiful: one design system (kit/design.css, kit/DESIGN.md).
5. It never stops improving (≥10 unticked items always in the backlog).
6. The teacher is a source, not an infallible authority: everything checked, his version always shown beside the check, every entry badged.
